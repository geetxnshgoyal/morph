Morph
Mac iPhone What it does Download

Drag a file, tap a key, drop it on the wheel.

Morph converts, shrinks and cleans up files right where they are, on your Mac, iPhone and Android. It's free, and nothing is ever uploaded.

Download for Mac iPhone and Android

macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

Try it: press Shift, Command, Control or Option, or tap a key above.

On the Mac, the wheel comes to your cursor.

While you drag files from Finder, the desktop or any app, tap a wheel's keys. That wheel opens right under the cursor; drop on an action and the result appears next to the original.

  • ⇧Convert images, video, audio, documents and data to the format you need.
  • ⌘Tools compress, resize, strip location data, blur faces and read text.
  • ⌃Recipes run several steps in one drop, like resize and convert to WEBP.
  • ⌥Actions send with AirDrop, copy the path, move, or open Terminal there.

Tap another wheel's keys to switch mid-drag, or press Esc to close. Every key and combination can be changed in Settings.

On iPhone, it's a Shortcuts app too.

Open files from any share sheet, or let your shortcuts do the work. Every action runs on the device and hands its results to the next step.

  • Convert Images
  • Compress Files
  • Remove Metadata
  • Extract Text
  • Read QR Codes and Barcodes
  • Merge PDFs
  • Run any Morph action or recipe
  • Open in Morph

"Remove location from photos with Morph."

Works with Siri, Spotlight and the Action button. Press and hold the app icon to convert photos straight away.

What's on the wheels

Only the actions that fit the files you're holding show up.

⇧Convert

  • JPG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, BMP
  • Images into one PDF
  • MP4, MOV, WEBM, MKV, GIF
  • M4A, WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS
  • DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, Markdown, PDF
  • SRT and VTT, CSV, TSV and JSON

⌘Tools

  • Compress images, video and PDFs
  • Resize
  • Remove metadata and location
  • Blur faces, hide personal info
  • Extract text, read QR codes
  • Merge and split PDFs
  • Zip and extract archives

⌃Recipes

  • Web Image
  • Under 1 MB
  • Share-Safe Photo
  • Your own, saved from the command line

⌥Actions

  • AirDrop, Messages, Mail
  • Copy, or copy the path
  • Move to or copy to a folder
  • Open Terminal there

Free, private and careful with your files.

Nothing leaves your device

Every conversion runs locally. There are no accounts, no uploads and no tracking.

Originals stay untouched

Results are saved beside the original with a new name, never on top of it.

Any job can be undone

Morph keeps a history; undo moves that job's results to the Trash.

Build instructions 
# The command-line tool ships inside the Mac app.
morph actions photo.heic              # what fits this file
morph run convert.webp --max 1600 photo.heic
morph run "recipe.Under 1 MB" scans/*.pdf
morph watch ~/Downloads tool.strip-metadata
